Student-run TQFT Seminar at IUPUI

The seminar meets on Tuesday at 10:30 am in LD 265, unless otherwise stated.

The organisers are Virgil Chan, and Filipp Uvarov.

The faculty advisor is Professor Vitaly Tarasov.

There is no particular plan, as the subject is new for organisers.

Our major references for this seminar is

  1. Michael F. Atiyah. Topological Quantum Field Theory.
  2. Daniel Dugger. Quantum Theory for Topologists.
  3. Nicolai Reshetikhin and Vladimir G. Turaev . Invariants of 3-Manifolds via Link Polynomials and Quantum Groups.

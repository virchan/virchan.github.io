IUPUI Student-run Algebraic Geometry Seminar

Together with Kang Lu, Chenliang Huang, Andrei Prokhorov, and Filipp Uvarov, I am organising a seminar on Algebraic Geometry at IUPUI. Feel free to send us an e-mail if you are interested in more information.

Spring 2017: We will focus on the theory of schemes, and algebraic groups.

Fall 2016: We introduced the notion of sheaves. In particular, we showed that presheaves of abelian groups on a topological space form an abelian category, and defined sheafification. We also covered some basics of affine algebraic varieties.

I tackle unforeseen and unsolved challenges every day, and I enjoy applying my training 
as a mathematician along with my Python programming skills to run experiments, analyse 
problems.

I'm currently working as a consultant in the San Francisco Bay Area, leveraging open-source libraries from 
the [Scientific Python](https://scientific-python.org/) ecosystem to build production-grade solutions for my clients.
Previously, I worked as a Forward Deployed Engineer at [Union.ai](https://www.union.ai/).
Over time, I've developed specialisations in Machine Learning, Predictive Data Analysis,
Open Source Development, and ML/AI Platform Engineering.

I'm a core contributor to [<code>scikit-learn</code>](https://scikit-learn.org), where 
I collaborate with the community to provide simple and 
efficient tools for accurate data analysis and machine learning. As a member of 
`scikit-learn`'s Maintainers Team, I also help triage issues 
and support new contributors in getting familiar with the codebase and contribution 
process through discussions and pull request reviews.

Previously, I studied mathematics at the 
[University of California, Davis](https://www.ucdavis.edu/), and
[Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/).

### Want to Work with Me?

I'm excited by opportunities where I can help solve complex technical challenges, especially in data science, 
machine learning, or open-source development, with an emphasis on creating tools that empower others to excel.
My [open-source contributions](https://github.com/virchan) reflect my focus on building solutions
that stand the test of time, both technically and in terms of community trust.

I work best with teams that have the confidence to welcome diverse perspectives and the imagination to explore
multiple paths to a solution. The most rewarding collaborations I've had were with people who already had
strong ideas, but were curious enough to test them against new ones. Because innovation rarely comes from doing
exactly what we've always done.

The teams I click with tend to measure success not just by delivery dates, but by the depth of thought
behind the work, the quality of the results, and the long-term value to the people who rely on it.
If you share that mindset, I think we'd get along famously.

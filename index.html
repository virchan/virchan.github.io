I am currently serving as the secretary for the AMS Student Chapter of IUPUI.

Together with Chenliang Huang, Kang Lu, Andrei Prokhorov, and Filipp Uvarov, I am organising a seminar on Algebraic Geometry at IUPUI. Feel free to send us an e-mail if you are interested in more information.

I am interested in Algebraic Topology, and Algebraic \(K\)-theory in general. More specifically, I am interested, or hoping to learn more about the followings:

  1. computations of \(K\)-theories,
  2. Assembly Maps in Algebraic \(K\)-theory and the Farrell-Jones Conjecture,
  3. Topological \(K\)-theory of infinite CW-complexes,
  4. Topological Cyclic Homology.

Currently, I am studying Waldhausen's \(S_{\bullet}\)-construction for Algebraic \(K\)-theory.
